# Day-by-day coverage first (requested by the user on 2026-10-15)

Goal: first fill in the dates within the specified research period that have not yet been queried, then continue pagination for the same day; the total number of items is no longer used as a substitute indicator of coverage completeness. The research period cutoff is still 2026-10-14, and download times are recorded separately.

- Reddit: only intervals not yet completed are split at UTC midnight; days are rotated. Intervals that hit the API's 100-item return cap are bisected and placed at the end of the queue, and collection continues until they are finished, with no cap on total requests or items. The monitoring window since 2025 has priority in the first round. Completed intervals and raw files are left untouched. A complete query of the community archive does not mean Reddit itself is complete.
- Media Cloud: from 2025-01-01 to the cutoff, both collections are queried day by day by publication date. For each date, one page is fetched first, and later pages are then rotated; dates with no downloaded stories yet have priority. Same-day requests with start=end have actually succeeded, and the program verifies that the publication date of every returned record matches. In the official service code, pagination over a whole window is sorted by indexed_date, so the earlier total item counts cannot prove coverage by publication date. The old window queries are paused, and the daily state file records the new task. The old batches and the new daily batches may overlap; the completeness table counts after deduplicating by story ID, and the raw data are not modified.
- YouTube: since 2023, for the channel ranges truncated by uploads, new day-by-day official search queries are added, first giving different dates a chance to be queried, then going deeper on a particular day or channel. A successful search with no results only means that query had no hits; it does not guarantee that no relevant video exists on the platform that day. Comment downloads are scheduled by the cumulative number of pages obtained per video publication date, filling in less-covered dates first; newly discovered videos are added every minute. The actual comment dates are counted separately; video date coverage is never the same as comment date coverage. There is no API for filtering comments by date, so pagination must continue, and complete daily comments across the whole platform cannot be promised. The quarterly samples for 2010–2022 in the original plan have not been completed.
- NYT, Guardian: the monthly batches already obtained are kept, and the daily distribution is checked by article publication date; completing a monthly batch does not mean there are stories every day, nor that the provider has updated the current month up to the cutoff.
- Surveys, macro: checked against the official release frequency, the expected observation periods, release dates, and historical vintages; monthly/quarterly values are not copied into daily values. Market closures, official suspension of releases, no data at the source site, and not yet downloaded are recorded separately.
- The existing access obstacles for GDELT and media ratings have not gone away because of this scheduling adjustment. Ratings are not daily-frequency data.

Day-by-day status table: daily_coverage.csv; summary: daily_coverage_summary.json. This is a timestamped snapshot and can be refreshed with src/collect/daily_coverage.py. Only counts, dates, and query completeness are checked; no content analysis, annotation, or hash verification is done.

Run state: reddit_collection_state.json, youtube_comment_text_state.json, youtube_daily_search_state.json, mediacloud_daily_state.json. The old supervisor state has been marked superseded_by_daily_coverage_workers. Actual API quotas, disabled comments, access errors, and so on are recorded as they are, and limits are not circumvented.

Basis for verification: the official Media Cloud client https://github.com/mediacloud/api-client/blob/main/mediacloud/api.py and the official service https://github.com/mediacloud/web-search/blob/main/mcweb/backend/search/views.py .
