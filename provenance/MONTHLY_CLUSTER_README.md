# Monthly text collection status table

The current main table is `monthly_cluster_status.csv`; the summary and run status are in `monthly_cluster_summary.json`. The fixed list is `monthly_cluster_plan.json`. The report is refreshed each time a sample day is completed and when the worker exits; this is not a scheduled task. The report reads only local data.

- Media Cloud: local 38379429, 2025-03 to 2026-09; national 34412234, 2025-09 to 2026-09. 2 days are drawn per collection per month, and a drawn day counts as complete only after its pagination is complete. Earlier complete months, complete dates, and the October portion up to the 14th are listed separately. Volume indicators use the existing daily search counts, and the snapshot can change as the source updates.
- Reddit: 3 UTC dates per month for economy and Economics in 2015–2024; personalfinance continues 3 days per month since 2010. Priority goes first to 9 recent personalfinance days, then the core subreddits for 2017–2024 and 2015–2016, and finally the remaining historical personalfinance. Half-day intervals can be reused, but a day is not counted as complete until the whole day is filled in.
- Each source independently uses Python `random.Random(20261015)` to draw dates without replacement in ascending month order; for the current month, draws are only made before the cutoff. Dates are fixed before download and are not redrawn because of inventory, speed, or the number of records on that day.
- This is day-level cluster sampling: records from the same day enter the sample together; it is not simple random sampling of individual stories/posts. Existing non-random inventory is listed separately and is not automatically mixed into the random sample.
- `planned_sample_days` / `completed_sample_days` are the current sample-day plan; `sample_days_reused` are dates that were drawn and were already complete; `sample_days_newly_completed` are dates filled in during this round.
- `sample_unique_records` counts only unique IDs within complete sample days; dates still being paginated are not counted. `sample_plan_completion` is the completion rate of the sample-day plan.
- `retained_other_full_days` / `retained_other_full_day_records` are complete dates and records in the old inventory that were not drawn, including dates outside the new backfill range; they are listed separately from the sample records.
- For Media Cloud, `retained_prior_month_inventory_records` is the monthly deduplicated count from the earlier audit of the raw inventory; complete months are based on an independent audit provided by the user. It may include records from other complete days, must not be added again, and is not guaranteed to agree with the newer count snapshot.
- `source_query_count` is the monthly count actually returned by the source; it is left empty when Reddit has not been queried. `count_snapshot_days` is the number of dates included in the Media Cloud denominator; 2026-10 only runs to the 14th.
- `observed_sample_fraction` is the number of records in complete sample days divided by the monthly search count; it **is not the collection completeness rate for the whole month**. Extra inventory and unfinished dates are not included in the numerator.
- Reddit does not collect author fields and keeps `retrieved_on`; scores and comment counts are snapshots at the time the archive crawled them and are not comparable across years.
- The old `sampling_monthly_status.csv` / `sampling_summary.json` belong to the stopped daily/half-day plan. The originals and the old reports are kept, without hash verification.

Media Cloud uses the existing daily search counts to estimate the remaining pages. Until all monthly counts are obtained, Reddit can only report a lower bound on the number of requests and time scenarios under explicit assumptions; it cannot guarantee that the whole project will finish within a few hours. After 5 consecutive transient errors it stops and marks the issue as needing the user to resolve it, without retrying indefinitely.

## Implemented personalfinance time-window rule (replaces the whole-day sampling description above)

The user has approved implementing personalfinance_window_design.json: each month still keeps the original three random dates, but only a circular time window with a random start on each date is fully pulled. The window length is calculated from the actual monthly count N so that the expected number of records per month is about 150; when a count is missing, no fake denominator is set. The random stream, selected intervals, probabilities, and count snapshots are all frozen and archived. The original list of complete whole days and the original files are still kept, and they do not become part of the new window sample.

Rows with sample_unit=circular_time_window use planned_sample_windows / completed_sample_windows / sample_windows_reused / sample_windows_newly_completed. For these rows, planned_sample_days / completed_sample_days are both 0, meaning the new design has no "complete whole day" target; it does not mean the old whole-day inventory has disappeared. The general planned_sample_dates / completed_sample_dates indicate completion of the sampling unit for the corresponding dates. target_expected_monthly_records, window_length_seconds, marginal_inclusion_probability, and selected_time_intervals record the design parameters.

retained_full_days_including_selected_dates / retained_full_day_records_including_selected_dates list separately the source's existing whole-day inventory, including dates drawn by the new time windows; this inventory overlaps with sample_unique_records and must not be added to it. The original 31 complete personalfinance days and the 9 days filled in earlier this round are all kept. When a date has only its complete selected window, the whole-day inventory count does not increase.

Provided that the actual count snapshot is stable and the source's interval API is exhaustive, every post has the same marginal inclusion probability, (3/D)*(L/86400). Records in the same time window enter the sample together, so this is still not simple random sampling of individual posts. The actual number of items per month can be above or below 150; randomness is not altered by truncation, redrawing, or padding. The automatic report refresh logic and the stop-on-failure rule are unchanged.

## Prioritize the complete personalfinance inventory

The full-month inventory intervals for 2025-01 to 2025-10 have been verified as complete and marked reuse_inventory_priority; the source aggregate monthly counts are filled in first, and then window samples are generated directly under the established date, random start, and length rules. These months need no new text requests. Review evidence is in personalfinance_inventory_reuse_audit.json. The source's new counts and the old inventory's deduplicated counts may differ because of snapshots; both are kept separately, without modifying the originals or fabricating denominators.

## Unified monthly count timeout rule (approved by the user)

After 5 consecutive timeouts on a full-month aggregate (including source 422 Timeout, read/connect timeouts, and gateway 504), non-overlapping UTC partitions of at most 7 days each are created automatically; the last segment of a month may be shorter than 7 days, and the cutoff month does not go past the research cutoff. The monthly denominator is summed and published only after all segments are obtained; segments already obtained are reused from the checkpoint, and the full month is not requested again. If a segment still fails 5 times in a row, the process stops and asks, and it must not keep subdividing indefinitely. 403 access denied and 429 rate limiting do not trigger this rule; the original access and rate-limit handling still applies.

The rule is saved in reddit_monthly_count_timeout_policy.json and in the state file; the source originals, counts, and intervals for the segments are in monthly_count_partitions. This rule resolved the count blockage for personalfinance 2012-11; it does not change the random dates, the time windows, the expected 150 items per month, or the author field boundary.
