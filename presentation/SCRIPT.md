## 3. Three explanations  (≈ 20 s)

Three explanations are possible: real pressures the headline numbers miss, like high prices; the media; or a problem with the survey or the model. [CLICK] We fixed all our tests in advance, including eight results that would count against the media story.

> 可能的解释有三种：一是头条数据没有反映的真实压力，比如物价太高；二是媒体；三是调查或模型本身的问题。[点击] 所有检验都是事先定好的，其中包括八条一旦出现就不利于媒体解释的结果。

## 5. H2 · Measuring the media  (≈ 29 s)

Now the media. We collected about 167,000 news items, posts and comments. An LLM coded over 11,000, and two people labeled 360 by hand to check it. [CLICK] On tone, the LLM agrees with humans almost as well as they agree with each other, and about twice as well as word lists. [CLICK] Swapping political names showed no sign of bias.

> 再看媒体。我们收集了约 16.7 万条新闻、帖子和评论，用大语言模型编码了其中一万一千多条，并请两个人独立手工标注了 360 条来检验。[点击] 在语气上，模型和人的一致程度几乎和两个人之间一样高，大约是词典法的两倍。[点击] 把政治人物的名字互换之后，也没有发现偏差。

## 6. H2 · What coverage shows  (≈ 22 s)

Coverage treats good and bad news alike, and only 2 of 68 checkable numbers were wrong. [CLICK] What changed is blame: under Trump’s second term, 40 percent of items blame the White House. [CLICK] But tariffs are his own decisions, so blame is not proof of bias.

> 报道对好消息和坏消息的处理差不多，能核对的 68 个数字里只有 2 个是错的。[点击] 变化最大的是归咎：特朗普第二任期内，40% 的报道把问题归咎于白宫。[点击] 但关税本来就是总统自己的决定，所以归咎不能证明有偏差。

The key test: does news tone help forecast sentiment? Each dot is one version of the test. [CLICK] Added to the economy model, tone helps a little. [CLICK] But with household costs and politics included, none of 20 versions is significant. [CLICK] At most, tone explains about 5 percent of the gap.
